== mission: 108 | difficulty: Inferno | class: any | source: https://gameannaijo.com/edf6_kasegi/
Tactical Aircraft Operation - the classic Inferno weapon farm
A large battle where EDF NPCs clash with an enemy army. The friendly units are
very strong, so you can stay behind the tanks, let them do the killing, and
collect what drops.
Japanese guides call this the gateway mission for farming top-level weapons.

== mission: 36 | difficulty: Easy | class: any | source: https://gameannaijo.com/edf6_armor/
Final Resistance - armor farm with endless enemies
Enemies keep spawning forever, so the mission never ends on its own and you can
keep collecting armor crates. Guides recommend running it on EASY.
This is the one usually mentioned for idle or low-effort armor farming.

== mission: 35 | difficulty: any | class: any | source: https://gameline.jp/edf6/kasegi/
The Crumbling Town - fast, clean loops
Only green ants spawn, so there is nothing unusual to deal with. It repeats
quickly and predictably, which is what matters when you care about runs per
hour rather than difficulty.

== mission: 117 | difficulty: any | class: Wing Diver | source: https://steamcommunity.com/app/2291060/discussions/0/4766584664415927379/
Base 228 in Peril - pylons, with Wing Diver
From the Steam discussion: destroy one pylon to trigger the five-pylon stage,
keep two or three of them close, and let the NPCs kill the ants.
The poster reports around 1000 armor per hour. Not verified by us.

== mission: 136 | difficulty: Easy | class: Ranger | source: https://gameranx.com/features/id/505186/article/edf6-8-best-levels-to-farm-inferno-weapons-armor/
The Giant God Comes Crashing - armor crates on Easy
Gameranx reports roughly 1000 armor crates per hour with Ranger on Easy
difficulty. Not verified by us.

== mission: any | difficulty: any | class: any | source: https://gameannaijo.com/edf6_kasegi/
General tips from the Japanese guides
Wide-area weapons such as rocket launchers shorten every run because they clear
whole groups at once.
Four-player multiplayer is far faster than solo.
For armor, use the lowest difficulty that still keeps enemies coming: what
matters is the number of crates, not the weapon level they roll.

== mission: any | difficulty: any | class: any | source: (computed by this mod)
How this crosses with your own progress
The Farming tab ranks missions by the chance that a crate gives you a weapon you
do NOT have yet, using your save. These strategies tell you where farming is
comfortable; that tab tells you where it is still worth something to you.
Read both: a comfortable mission whose pool you have already completed gives you
nothing.
